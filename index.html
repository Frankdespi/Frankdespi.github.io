    Featured project

    Reputation & Feedback Intelligence Engine

    My capstone for the TS Academy AI & Automation program. Two connected n8n workflows turn a customer feedback form into a scored, routed, and logged case. It is built around a car care chain case study and uses sample data only.

    Workflow 01: send the feedback request
    When a job is marked complete in Google Sheets, the customer receives an email with a pre-filled Google Form link.
    Workflow 02: score and route feedback
    Each form reply is scored by OpenAI, routed, drafted if needed, and logged. Urgent cases alert a manager on Telegram and by email.
    • Unreadable or low-confidence results go to a person instead of being acted on.
    • AI-drafted replies are saved for review and never sent automatically.
    • Repeat complaints are detected and escalated.
    • Every outcome is logged with its score, route, and timestamp.

    n8n, OpenAI API, JavaScript Code nodes, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, Telegram

    Also built

    Sales & Inventory Automation

    A small juice business was tracking everything by hand. The owner now logs sales, batches, and costs in Google Sheets, picking the flavour and size from dropdowns. The spreadsheet does the arithmetic, and n8n does the watching, in six lanes on one canvas.

    • A Telegram alert when a flavour or its stickers run low.
    • An 8 pm summary by Telegram and email: bottles sold, revenue, top seller, and which flavours and sizes were sold.
    • A 6 pm nudge if nothing has been logged yet.
    • A Sunday roll-up of revenue, costs, profit, and flavours sold, saved to the sheet and sent to the owner by Telegram and email.
    • An error alert, and a weekly backup to Google Drive.

    I also wrote a short daily guide so the owner can run it without me.

    n8n, Google Sheets, Telegram, Gmail, Google Drive, JavaScript Code nodes

    My First AI Agent

    An n8n AI agent that takes one Telegram prompt, searches the web, then messages and emails me the answer. A Telegram trigger feeds an AI Agent node with an OpenAI chat model, Simple Memory, a SerpApi search tool, and a Gmail tool.

    The main lesson was that an agent will not chain tools on its own. The system prompt has to say so explicitly: search first, then email the result. Without that instruction, the agent stops after one tool.

    n8n AI Agent, OpenAI Chat Model, Simple Memory, SerpApi, Gmail, Telegram
